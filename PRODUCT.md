# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Wireless R&D engineers and AI practitioners in enterprise/telecom settings. They visit the community during work hours to: stay current on AI developments relevant to wireless tech, find learning resources, share practical agent/tool implementations, and discover reusable extensions/skills/commands for their workflow. They value information density and scan quickly.

## Product Purpose

A community platform that connects wireless R&D professionals around AI. It aggregates intelligence (news, papers, tools), curates courses, hosts forum discussions, and operates an agent marketplace for extensions, skills, MCPs, subagents, and CLI commands. Success means members find actionable knowledge and reusable tools with minimal friction.

## Brand Personality

Professional, clean, collaborative. Sufficient tech-savviness to signal credibility without being cold. Understated design confidence: small thoughtful details over loud decoration. Information clarity is the first priority; aesthetic delight is secondary and should never compete with readability.

## Anti-references

- Avoid heavy SaaS marketing tropes: hero metric blocks, oversized gradient illustrations, endless identical icon+heading+text card grids
- Avoid low-density layouts where one screen holds a single call-to-action and little else
- Avoid PPT-style decorative media (clipart-style 3D illustrations, generic "tech" imagery)
- Avoid gimmicky glassmorphism, animated particle backgrounds, or gratuitous motion that distracts from content
- Avoid side-stripe accent borders and gradient text

## Design Principles

1. **Content first, chrome second** — UI elements should recede so information stands out
2. **Scan-friendly density** — engineers skim; hierarchy and spacing must support rapid scanning
3. **Consistent affordances** — same interaction patterns across intelligence, courses, forum, and market reduce cognitive load
4. **Subtle craft signals confidence** — small refined details (precise corners, restrained color, purposeful whitespace) communicate quality better than ornament
5. **Every pixel earns its place** — no decorative elements without a functional or emotional reason

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Ensure sufficient color contrast for badges and category tags. Support keyboard navigation for all interactive elements.
